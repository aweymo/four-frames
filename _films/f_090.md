A little like _The Narrow Margin_ in the way it's only a noir in the dutch angles and the pastiche of the dialogue but otherwise a very brisk, bubbly film. The title is a feint, actually about razor sharp badass Ann Sheridan attempting to find her loopy, not terribly talented artist husband who was the only witness to a murder. Like _Thieves’ Highway_, the amount of juice this gains by setting the action on location in a San Francisco that largely doesn’t exist anymore is incredible. Well deployed twist, excellent stem winder conclusion and unexpected subplot of a sparked friendship between a hapless detective and a cute dog.
